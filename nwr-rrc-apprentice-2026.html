Railway Apprenticeship 2026

North Western Railway (NWR) Act Apprentice Recruitment 2026 (Notification No. 04/2026)

ਛੋਟੀ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ: ਰੇਲਵੇ ਰਿਕਰੂਟਮੈਂਟ ਸੈੱਲ (RRC), ਉੱਤਰੀ ਪੱਛਮੀ ਰੇਲਵੇ (NWR), ਜੈਪੁਰ ਵੱਲੋਂ ਸਾਲ 2026-27 ਲਈ ਵੱਖ-ਵੱਖ ਵਣਜਾਂ (Trades) ਵਿੱਚ ਐਕਟ ਅਪ੍ਰੈਂਟਿਸ (Act Apprentices) ਦੀਆਂ ਕੁੱਲ 2008 ਖਾਲੀ ਸਲਾਟਸ 'ਤੇ ਭਰਤੀ ਲਈ ਆਨਲਾਈਨ ਅਰਜ਼ੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਮੰਗ ਕੀਤੀ ਗਈ ਹੈ।

📌 Important Details / ਮੁੱਖ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ:

Recruiting Body Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), North Western Railway, Jaipur
Total Training Slots 2,008 Posts
Starting Date of Online Application 07.10.2026
Last Date to Apply Online 06.11.2026 (Up to 23:59 Hrs)
Selection Mode Based on Merit (Average of Matriculation & ITI marks)

🎓 Eligibility Criteria (ਯੋਗਤਾ ਅਤੇ ਉਮਰ ਸੀਮਾ):

Educational Qualification Candidate must have passed 10th class examination or its equivalent with minimum 50% marks, and also possess National Trade Certificate (NTC/ITI) in the notified trade issued by NCVT/SCVT.
Age Limit (as on 06.11.2026) Between 15 years to 24 years. (Age relaxations applicable for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen as per rules).

💰 Application Fee (ਫੀਸ ਵੇਰਵਾ):

General / OBC / EWS ₹100/-
SC / ST / PwBD / Women Nil (No Fee)

🏢 Participating Divisions / Units (ਯੂਨਿਟਾਂ ਦੇ ਨਾਮ):

1. DRM Office, Ajmer
2. DRM Office, Bikaner
3. DRM Office, Jaipur
4. DRM Office, Jodhpur
5. B.T.C. Carriage, Ajmer
6. B.T.C. Loco, Ajmer
7. Carriage Workshop, Bikaner
8. Carriage Workshop, Jodhpur
