🎓 Eligibility Criteria (ਯੋਗਤਾ ਅਤੇ ਉਮਰ ਸੀਮਾ):

1. Educational Qualification (ਪੜ੍ਹਾਈ):
- Minimum X Standard (Matriculation) examination conducted by a recognized Board/Institute.
- Maximum XII Standard / 10+2 examination conducted by a recognized Board/Institute. (Candidates with higher qualifications than 10+2 are not eligible).

2. Age Limit (ਉਮਰ ਸੀਮਾ - As on 01.10.2026):
- Minimum Age: 18 Years
- Maximum Age: 27 Years
- Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC (NCL), PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and widows/divorced women as per Government of India rules.
