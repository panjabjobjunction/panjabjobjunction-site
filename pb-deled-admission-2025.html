Punjab D.El.Ed. Diploma Course Admission 2025-27 (SCERT Punjab)

ਛੋਟੀ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ: State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Punjab ਵੱਲੋਂ ਸਰਕਾਰੀ (DIETs) ਅਤੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਈਵੇਟ ਐਫੀਲੀਏਟਿਡ ਸੰਸਥਾਵਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ 2-ਸਾਲਾ D.El.Ed. (Diploma in Elementary Education) ਕੋਰਸ (Session 2025-27) ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਖਲੇ ਲਈ ਕੰਬਾਈਡ ਇੰਟਰੈਂਸ ਟੈਸਟ ਅਤੇ ਕਾਉਂਸਲਿੰਗ ਪਾਲਿਸੀ ਜਾਰੀ ਕੀਤੀ ਗਈ ਹੈ.

📌 Overview / ਮੁੱਖ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ:

Conducting Body (ਮਹਿਕਮਾ) SCERT, Department of School Education, Punjab
Course Name (ਕੋਰਸ ਦਾ ਨਾਮ) 2-Year D.El.Ed. (Diploma in Elementary Education)
Academic Session 2025-2027 (Commences July 2025)
Total Seats (ਕੁੱਲ ਸੀਟਾਂ) 4,920 Counselling Quota Seats + 2,280 Management Quota Seats
Selection Process Combined Entrance Test (CET) Merit & Centralized Online Counselling
Residential Status Candidate must be a resident of Punjab State

🎓 Eligibility Criteria (ਯੋਗਤਾ ਅਤੇ ਉਮਰ ਸੀਮਾ):

1. Educational Qualification (ਪੜ੍ਹਾਈ):
- Graduation degree with minimum 50% marks for General category and 45% marks for SC/BC/PH candidates from a recognized institution.
- Passed Punjabi subject up to Matriculation level.
- Passed English, Hindi, Punjabi, Science, Social Studies, and Mathematics in Class 10th.

2. Age Limit (ਉਮਰ ਸੀਮਾ):
- Maximum Age: 37 years for General category candidates.
- Age Relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST and 10 years for Physically Handicapped candidates.

💰 Application & Course Fee Structure (ਫੀਸ ਵੇਰਵਾ):

Online Application Fee (General / BC / OBC / EWS / Sports) ₹1,350/- (Including GST)
Online Application Fee (SC / ST / PH) ₹750/- (Including GST)
Application Fee (Ex-Servicemen Self) Nil (No Fee)
DIET Fee (Government Institutes) ₹8,500/- per year
Private College Fee (Counselling Quota) ₹40,000/- per year
Private College Fee (Management Quota) ₹44,000/- per year
