2. Possession of a valid driving license for Light & Heavy Motor Vehicles.
3. Knowledge of Motor Mechanism (ability to remove minor defects).
4. Experience of driving Light & Heavy Motor Vehicles for at least 3 years.[cite: 8]
Age Limit (as on 30.11.2026) • UR & EWS: 18 to 27 years.
