Apprenticeship Training 2027-29

Naval Dockyard Karwar & Naval Aircraft Yard Goa Apprenticeship Recruitment

ਛੋਟੀ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ: ਭਾਰਤੀ ਜਲ ਸੈਨਾ (Indian Navy) ਅਧੀਨ ਨਵਾਲ ਡੌਕਯਾਰਡ, ਕਾਰਵਾਰ (ਕਰਨਾਟਕਾ) ਅਤੇ ਨਵਾਲ ਏਅਰਕ੍ਰਾਫਟ ਯਾਰਡ, ਗੋਆ ਵਿਖੇ ਸੈਸ਼ਨ 2027-29 ਲਈ ਵੱਖ-ਵੱਖ ਟਰੇਡਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਪ੍ਰੈਂਟਿਸਸ਼ਿਪ ਟ੍ਰੇਨਿੰਗ ਵਾਸਤੇ ਆਈ.ਟੀ.ਆਈ. (ITI) ਅਤੇ ਯੋਗ ਉਮੀਦਵਾਰਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਆਨਲਾਈਨ ਅਰਜ਼ੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਮੰਗ ਕੀਤੀ ਗਈ ਹੈ।

🎓 Educational Qualification (ਯੋਗਤਾ):

- ITI Trades: 10th Standard (SSC) Pass + ITI (NCVT/SCVT) in the relevant designated trade.
- Non-ITI Trades (Crane Operator, Forger & Heat Treater): 10th Standard Pass.
- Rigger Trade: 8th Standard Pass.

💰 Stipend (ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ - ਪ੍ਰਤੀ ਮਹੀਨਾ):

- ITI Certificate Holders: ₹9,600/- per month.
- Crane Operator / Forger / Rigger: Ranges from ₹3,400/- to ₹9,020/- per month as per training duration and rules.

📝 Selection Process & Exam Pattern (ਚੋਣ ਪ੍ਰਕਿਰਿਆ):

1. Preliminary Merit List: Based on aggregate percentage of marks in ITI and 10th standard (or 8th/10th for non-ITI).
2. Written Examination: Shortlisted candidates (1:20 ratio) will be called for an OMR-based written exam in Jan 2027 at Karwar.
  • Syllabus (100 Marks / 2 Hours / No Negative Marking): Mathematics (35 Marks), General Science (35 Marks), General Knowledge (30 Marks).
3. Document Verification & Medical Examination: Final merit list based on written exam marks followed by medical fitness test.

🚀 How to Apply (ਅਪਲਾਈ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਕਰੀਏ):

1. Register online on the official NAPS portal: www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
2. Search for establishment "Naval Dockyard, Karwar" and apply for the desired trade.
3. Download the completed candidate profile from the portal.
4. Send the hard copy of the profile along with self-attested copies of 10th/8th certificate, ITI mark sheets, category certificate, Aadhar card, and choice certificate by Speed/Registered Post to:
"The Officer-in-Charge, Dockyard Apprentice School, Naval Dockyard, Naval Base, Karwar, Karnataka - 581 308" within 30 days.
